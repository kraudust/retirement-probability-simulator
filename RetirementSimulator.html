Every dollar figure is in today's money. Enter investment returns the normal way (before inflation); the model takes inflation out for you. A balance of $2,000,000 at age 70 means $2,000,000 of what a dollar buys today.

    
This is a planning model, not financial advice and not a prediction. Use it to compare choices against each other rather than to trust any single number. Every input has a ? button explaining what it means and how to pick a value.
